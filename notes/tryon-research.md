# Outfit try-on: what other apps do, and what Glow Studio's Try on does (2026-10-31)

## What the others do
| App | How you give the outfit | Your photo | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Try On (Search/Shopping) | "Try it on" button on tops, bottoms, dresses in shopping results | One full-body photo or selfie, saved for reuse. Tips: good light, plain background, stand tall, fitted clothes | Save, share, "Recently tried" tray |
| Google Doppl | Photo or screenshot of any outfit (friend, thrift shop, social) | Full-body photo, or an AI model | Image, can turn into a short video; saved looks; shoppable feed |
| YouCam (Perfect Corp) | Upload a clothing picture or premade styles; choose Upper body / Lower body / Full body; "AI Wardrobe" closet sorted by type | Full body, straight pose, arms away, natural light | Several options to compare, save, share |
| Picsart AI Try On | Preset outfit categories; AI Replace (brush + words) | Front-facing selfie | One result |
| Facetune | Presets or your own words; colors, textures, patterns | Well lit; avoid groups and covered faces | Export and share. Subscription |
| Remini AI Outfits | Hundreds of preset items and colors | 8-12 selfies to train a model | Photo shoot: several poses, compare outfits |
| FASHN / Kling / Leffa (APIs) | Product photo (flat-lay, mannequin or worn); category top / bottom / one-piece | One person photo | 5-55 s; $0.04-0.10 per image; some change the face |

Common gaps: no check of your photo before you pay, faces drift, no way back to an earlier look without paying again, outfit photos not kept for next time.

## Glow Studio's Try on
- One "Try on" tool, first in the Looks tab (replaces Suit, Sundress and the separate outfit tool).
- Three ways to give the outfit: **Ideas** (13 looks, any of 8 colors), **Your photo** (photo, screenshot or Paste; kept in a small closet on the phone for next time), **Describe** (her own words).
- **What to change**: Auto, Top, Bottom or Full look (keeps the rest of her own outfit).
- **Free photo check** before any credit: warns when the photo shows little of the clothes, no person, or several people.
- **Her face stays hers**: if the AI moved the face, her own face goes back in (as Describe does).
- **Your looks**: every look made on the photo stays in a row under the tool; flip between them and apply any one, free. Discard no longer loses a paid look.
- Server: Nano Banana 2 first (best garment fidelity and identity in the Pro Edit test), then Nano Banana, then IDM-VTON (photo only, with the garment category). Still 1 credit, mode "outfit", no database change.
- The AI sample still shows the prepared Suit and Summer dress results, inside Try on.

Not built (ideas for later): short video of the look (Doppl), shop links, size advice, outfit from a web link.
