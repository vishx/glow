Glow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Glow is a photo editing app. This policy explains what information Glow handles, why, and the choices you have.

Photos you edit

  • On-phone tools (filters, Face and makeup tools such as smoothing, teeth whitening, slimming and eyeliner, Body tools, hair color, background blur and removal, the live beauty camera preview) process your photos entirely on your device. These photos are not uploaded.
  • Face, body and hair tools find the position of the eyes, lips, face outline, body and hair in the photo, on your device, only to apply the edit you chose. This data is never uploaded, stored, or used to identify anyone, and it is discarded as soon as the edit is done.
  • AI tools (such as Enhance, Restore, Faces, Retouch, Smile, Relight, hairstyles, outfits and outfit try-on, Styles, Magic erase, Expand, and the camera's Auto fix when it is turned on) send the photo you choose, and for outfit try-on the outfit photo you pick, to our AI processing provider, Replicate, Inc., which runs the model and returns the result. We do not use your photos to train models, sell them, or show them to anyone else. Processed images are kept by the provider only temporarily (typically up to one hour) so the app can download the result.
  • Photos you save go to your device's photo library. Glow does not keep a copy on our servers.

Your account

When you first open Glow, we create an anonymous account so we can count your AI edits and apply your subscription. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number. We store: a random account ID, your plan (free or Pro) and its expiry date, a log of which AI tools you used and when, and a record of any optional ads you finished. To stop abuse of free AI edits, we also store the internet (IP) address each AI edit came from and delete it within a few days.

Optional ads

In the phone app, when you run out of free AI edits, you can choose to watch a short ad to get one more. Ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which receives your device and network information to show and measure the ad, as described in Google's policy. We ask Google for non-personalized ads, and where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK) Glow asks for your consent first. Google tells us only that an ad was finished, so we can add your edit.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm your subscription status, which receives your anonymous account ID and your store purchase records.

Service providers

  • Supabase, Inc.: hosts our database and server functions.
  • Replicate, Inc.: runs the AI models for AI tools.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: manages subscription status.
  • Vercel, Inc.: hosts the Glow website.
  • Google LLC (AdMob): shows the optional ads in the phone app.

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the optional ads above, Glow has no advertising or tracking.

Camera and photo access

Glow asks for camera access to take photos and show live beauty effects, and for photo library access to open the photos you pick and save your results. You can change these permissions in your phone's settings at any time.

Deleting your data

In Glow, open Settings (⚙︎) and tap Delete my account and data. This permanently deletes your account, plan record, usage log and ad records. Cancel any active subscription in your App Store or Google Play settings as well.

Children

Glow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
