Glow

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Glow is a beauty camera and photo editing app. This policy explains what information Glow handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: your photos stay on your phone unless you use an AI tool, we never sell your data, and you can delete everything at any time.

Photos you edit

  • On-phone tools (the live beauty camera, filters, looks, face and makeup tools, background blur, crop and adjustments) process your photos entirely on your device. These photos are not uploaded.
  • Face tools find the position of the eyes, lips and face outline in the photo, on your device, only to apply the edit you chose. This data is never uploaded, stored, or used to identify anyone, and it is discarded when the edit is done.
  • AI tools (such as Enhance, Retouch, Restore, Colorize, Magic erase, Fill, AI backgrounds, Headshot and Describe) send the photo you choose, and any words you type for Describe, to our AI processing provider, Replicate, Inc., which runs the model and returns the result. We do not use your photos to train models, sell them, or show them to anyone. The provider keeps the images only temporarily (typically up to one hour) so the app can download the result.
  • Photos you save go to your device's photo library. Glow does not keep a copy on our servers.
  • Your current edit is kept in the app's storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off, until you discard it or start a new one. It is not uploaded.

Your account

When you first open Glow, we create an anonymous account so we can count your AI credits and apply your subscription. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number. We store: a random account ID, your plan (free or Pro) and its expiry date, a log of which AI tools you used and when, and a record of any optional ads you finished. To stop abuse of free AI credits, we also store the internet (IP) address each AI run came from and delete it within a few days.

App diagnostics

To find bugs and understand which features are used, the app sends a small number of events to our own database, linked to your anonymous account ID: when the app opens, when the Glow Pro screen is shown, purchases started or completed, which AI tool was started and whether it failed, and when you contact support. If the app hits an error, it sends the error message, the app version and your platform (iPhone, Android or web). These never include your photos, the words you type, or your contacts. We keep events for up to 180 days and error reports for up to 60 days. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking.

Support messages

If you use Contact support, we receive your message, the email address you give us (optional), your account ID, and basic app details (plan, credits left, app version, device type) so we can help. We keep support messages for up to 2 years and use your email only to reply.

Optional ads

In the phone app, when you run out of free AI credits, you can choose to watch a short ad for one more. Ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which receives your device and network information to show and measure the ad, as described in Google's policy. We ask Google for non-personalized ads, and where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK) Glow asks for your consent first. Google tells us only that an ad was finished, so we can add your credit.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm your subscription status; it receives your anonymous account ID and your store purchase records.

Service providers

  • Supabase, Inc.: hosts our database and server functions.
  • Replicate, Inc.: runs the AI models for AI tools.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: manages subscription status.
  • Vercel, Inc.: hosts the Glow website.
  • Google LLC (AdMob): shows the optional ads in the phone app.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us. Some are located in the United States, so your data may be processed there. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Camera and photo access

Glow asks for camera access to take photos and show live beauty effects, and for photo library access to open the photos you pick and save your results. You can change these permissions in your phone's settings at any time.

Your rights and deleting your data

In Glow, open Settings (⚙︎) and tap Delete my account and data. This permanently deletes your account, plan record, AI usage log, ad records and app events. Without the app, see deleting your Glow data. Cancel any active subscription in your App Store or Google Play settings as well.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may also have the right to access or correct your data, object to its use, or complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to make a request.

Children

Glow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

Contact

Use Contact support in Glow's Settings, or email glowphotoapp.support@gmail.com.
