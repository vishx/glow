Help and support

Answers to common questions. If you still need help, we're here.

Contact us
In Glow, open Settings (⚙︎) and tap Contact support. Your message reaches us with your account ID, so we can look into it quickly. You can also email glowphotoapp.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

AI credits

What are AI credits?

AI tools such as Enhance run on our servers, and each run uses credits: most tools use 1, some (like Fill, AI scene, Headshot and Describe) use 2. The app shows the cost before you apply a tool. Tools that run on your phone, like the beauty camera, filters, makeup and adjustments, are always free.

How many credits do I get?

Free: 3 credits a day for everyday AI tools, plus 3 one-time credits to try premium tools. In the phone app you can watch an optional short ad for 1 more a day. Glow Pro: 40 credits every week.

An AI tool failed. Did I lose a credit?

No. If an AI run fails or never starts, its credit is returned automatically. If something still looks wrong, contact us and we'll check your account.

Glow Pro and payments

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so you cancel in your store account. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

  • iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions
  • Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by the store that charged you.

  • iPhone: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: within 48 hours of purchase, request one in Google Play order history. After that, contact us and we'll help.
I paid but don't have Pro

Open the Glow Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you paid with. If Pro still doesn't show after a minute, contact us.

I have a new phone or reinstalled Glow

Glow doesn't use a login. Your subscription belongs to your Apple or Google account: tap Restore purchases on the Glow Pro screen to bring it back.

Photos and privacy

Do you keep my photos?

No. Photos you edit with on-phone tools never leave your phone. For AI tools, the photo goes to our AI provider only to make your result, and isn't kept beyond about an hour. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

In Glow, open Settings and tap Delete my account and data. If you no longer have the app, see deleting your Glow data.
