Delete your Glow Studio account and data

This page explains how to delete your account and what is deleted.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Glow Studio and tap Settings (⚙︎).
  2. Tap Delete my account and data, then Delete everything.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email glowphotoapp.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Glow Studio data". If you can, include your account ID (in Glow Studio: Settings › Contact support). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account and its ID
  • Your plan record and expiry date
  • Your ad rewards and app activity events
  • The link between you and your AI tool use, and the internet addresses it came from

What is kept

  • Support messages you sent, without the link to your account, for up to 2 years so we can answer follow-ups. Ask us and we'll delete them too.
  • App error reports, without the link to your account, for up to 60 days.
  • A count of AI tool runs (which tool, when, whether it worked) with no account ID or address, so we can track our costs.
  • A note that an account was deleted (plan, how old it was, how many AI runs), with no account ID.
  • Our copy of subscription events (renewals, cancellations, refunds), without the link to your account, for tax and accounting.
  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat, which they keep under their own policies for tax and accounting.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Photos are never stored on our servers, so there are none to delete. Photos you saved are in your phone's photo library.
