# Try on outfit photos: sources

Made 2026-11-03 with Glow Studio's own AI (the live `enhance` function, Nano Banana) from the
sample model in `../demo-v2/original.png` (AI-generated, not a real person):

1. A full-body photo of her in a white tee and jeans (`custom` mode on the portrait padded to 2:3).
2. That photo dressed in each outfit from `TRY_OUTFITS` in `tryon.js` with Try on (`outfit` mode,
   the outfit's own words and part), exactly as the app asks for it.
3. Cropped head-down around her (a top: 50% of the height, a full look: 60%), 192 × 258 WebP.

The full-size results, the base photo and the scripts are kept in the project files
(`glow-tryon-wardrobe/outfit-photos-full/`). A new outfit needs a new photo made the same way.
